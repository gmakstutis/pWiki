# Online Learning

## Definition
A mode of delivery through which learners complete all required elements of a course through an online delivery system (or an app), although they may participate in discussion forums or group projects involving interaction with each other and the instructor. Blended (hybrid) courses consist of both an online and a face-to-face component. Non-traditional students, those with lower GPAs, ethnic minority students, and first-generation students are more likely to enroll online.
