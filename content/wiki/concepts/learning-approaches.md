# Learning Approaches

## Definition

The ways in which students engage with learning tasks, distinguished from learning styles (persistent tendencies) and learning orientations (combinations of style and approach). The deep approach implies learning for understanding, while the surface approach implies learning to memorize facts. A student's approach to learning is only partly a function of general characteristics, since it can be modified by specific learning situations, including the perception of the relevance of the task, the attitudes of the lecturer, and the expected forms of assessment.
