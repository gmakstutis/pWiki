# Formative Assessment

## Definition

Assessment aimed at yielding results that are immediately useful in guiding subsequent action. Volkwein (2003) describes it as a "feedback loop in which teaching influences learning, learning influences outcomes, and assessment of outcomes is used to influence/improve teaching and, ultimately, learning." At the individual level, it involves giving regular feedback to students about their progress; at the program or institutional level, it facilitates program modifications and increased teaching effectiveness.
