# Design Education

## Definition

The teaching and learning of design, in which learning and teaching methods aim to balance the creative process with a critical awareness of more objective criteria in the development of a proposition. Each design outcome tends to be unique, non-repetitive and immanent in its conception and development. The learning process is characterized by continual dialogue, with students learning from sharing information with one another and instructors, and from the critiques of the jury members. The most important learning experience comes from self-reflection, a skill central to the acquisition of all design knowledge and skills. Design education can be considered as being in line with Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory.
