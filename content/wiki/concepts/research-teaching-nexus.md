# Research-Teaching Nexus

## Definition
The interconnection between research and teaching in higher education, traced back to Von Humboldt in the nineteenth century. Healey and Jenkins (2009) distinguish four ways of linking research and teaching: research-led (content focus, students as audience), research-oriented (process focus, students as audience), research-tutored (content focus, students as active participants), and research-based (process focus, students as active participants).
