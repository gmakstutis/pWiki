# Learning Gain

## Definition
The academic, professional and/or personal value added that higher education provides. Learning gain can be considered from the dual perspectives of 'distance travelled' and 'value-added' (Houreau McGrath et al., 2015). The 'distance travelled' aspect encapsulates personal development as well as the enhancement of knowledge, skills and competencies; the 'value-added' aspect is based on an 'exceeding expectation' ethos. Graduate employment is sometimes used as a proxy measure for learning gain. Measuring learning gain is contested, with obstacles including the appeal of complexity, a reluctance to justify, the question of whose learning gain, the "rubber ruler" of assessment, and politics.
