# Work Experience

## Definition
Experience of work that can contribute to higher educational standards and to the development of a flexible, highly-skilled and enterprising labour force. There are three main types: organised work experience that is part of a programme of study, organised work experience external to a programme, and ad hoc work experience external to a programme. Neither experience of work, nor any form of work experience, is intrinsically beneficial; quality depends upon its fitness for purpose as an aid to learning.
