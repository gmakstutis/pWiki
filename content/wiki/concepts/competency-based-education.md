# Competency-Based Education

## Definition
A form of flexible learning providing self-paced learning opportunities in which students can demonstrate their competencies in certain areas. In some cases, students can gain credit for prior learning by demonstrating competencies they already have — referred to as Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) in North American contexts and Accreditation of Prior Learning (APL) in UK and EU contexts. Both CBE and PLA are strategies for widening access to higher education for students with significant workplace experience.
