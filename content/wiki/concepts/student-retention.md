# Student Retention

## Definition

The extent to which students continue their studies and complete their programme of study. Theoretical models of student retention and transition are strongly influenced by Tinto's student integration theory, which emphasises social and academic integration in relation to a student's commitment to the institution. Students bring prior schooling, skills and abilities to university; institutions must establish student expectations and enable students to develop social and academic integration skills in both informal and formal ways. Barriers to retention include a lack of academic skills, difficulties with academic tutors, and the challenge of transitioning from school to university.
