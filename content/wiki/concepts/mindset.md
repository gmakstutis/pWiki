# Mindset

## Definition

A belief about whether human qualities, such as intelligence and personality, are fixed traits or can be cultivated through effort. The fixed mindset is the belief that qualities are carved in stone, creating an urgency to prove oneself over and over. The growth mindset is the belief that basic qualities can be cultivated through effort, creating a passion for learning and stretching oneself. The growth mindset allows people to thrive during challenging times, while the fixed mindset makes people into nonlearners who fear challenge and devalue effort. Mindsets are beliefs that can be changed.
