# Group Work

## Definition
Group projects and other forms of group work used as part of the assessment process in higher education. Group learning is widely recognised as beneficial, with academic, social, and practical benefits including development of critical skills, independence, enhanced motivation, and the development of skills such as collaboration, negotiation, and leadership. However, group work does not automatically benefit students; if it is to be successful, it needs to be facilitated and students need preparation and guidance.
