# Learning Outcomes

## Definition

The personal changes or benefits that follow as a result of learning, measured in terms of abilities or achievements. Otter (1992) defined learning outcomes as "what a learner knows or can do as a result of learning." Outcomes describe what the student actually achieves, as opposed to what the institution intends to teach. The term has its origins in outcomes-based education, a model of educational structuring involving the clear and explicit identification, statement, and assessment of student learning.
