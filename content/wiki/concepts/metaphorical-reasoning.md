# Metaphorical Reasoning

## Definition

The use of metaphor to structure and reframe a problem, allowing the transfer of concepts and ideas from remote domains to the problem at hand. Metaphors are not merely decorative but structure and organise thought. In design, metaphorical reasoning is a practical strategy for vague, ill-structured, and non-routine problems, and serves to stimulate a reflective dialogue with the design situation (Schön). The basic processes of metaphorical reasoning can be organised into three major phases: identification and retrieval, mapping and transference, and adaptation and application (Gentner, 1983).
