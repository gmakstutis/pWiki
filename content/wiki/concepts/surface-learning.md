# Surface Learning

## Definition

Rote learning, or more temporary learning, as opposed to deep learning with understanding. The surface approach is characterized by students who try simply to memorize parts of the content of teaching materials, accept ideas and information given without question, concentrate on memorizing facts without distinguishing underlying principles or patterns, and are influenced by assessment requirements.
