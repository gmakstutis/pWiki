# Peer Observation

## Definition

A process in which peers observe a tutor's performance through classroom observation and examination of instructional materials and course design, used as a tool for personal and professional development. It is regarded as developmental rather than summative: the observer is not there to assess teaching but to provide feedback enabling the observed to assess their own teaching. Observation is not a neutral, objective process — the observer, method, and instruments all impact the information obtained.
