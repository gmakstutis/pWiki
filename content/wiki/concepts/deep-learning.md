# Deep Learning

## Definition
Learning with understanding, as opposed to surface (rote) learning. The deep approach is characterized by students who seek to understand issues and interact critically with teaching materials, relate ideas to previous knowledge and experience, and examine the logic of arguments. Deep learning is not universally desirable — it may be necessary to adopt a surface approach given the nature of the knowledge to be acquired.
