# Social Justice

## Definition
A concept concerned with the fair and just distribution of resources, opportunities, and privileges in society, and with the patterning of inequality in relation to race, class, and gender. In education, social justice is often contrasted with narrow, technicised models of social mobility that reduce inequality to a technical issue. Education cannot compensate for society, but it can be a resource in the struggle for social justice.
