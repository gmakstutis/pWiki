# Widening Participation

## Definition

The policy and practice of expanding access to higher education for under-represented and disadvantaged groups. In England, Widening Participation (WP) has been seen as pivotal to the development of knowledge-based economies. However, while WP has widened access to HE, it has not systematically enabled relative upward social mobility, and the differences in participation associated with social background remain intact.
