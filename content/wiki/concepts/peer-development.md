# Peer Development

## Definition

A collaborative process of professional development in which colleagues identify an aspect of practice of mutual interest that they wish to develop, expanding the focus area to include aspects of professional practice other than those traditionally regarded as 'teaching', while removing the requirement for observation. It is designed to enable colleagues to develop their own practice in a meaningful way by engaging in dialogue with others about pedagogy, strengthening the emphasis on collaboration and mutual professional development.
