# Self-Regulated Learning

## Definition

Learning in which students take responsibility for the factors and conditions affecting their learning. Its six dimensions are motive (reasons or purpose for learning — why), methods (strategies for learning — how), time (prioritization, effective use of time — when), physical environment (a distractor-free study situation — where), social environment (help-seeking and collaboration — with whom), and performance (progress, reflection, and goals — what). Self-regulation is closely related to metacognition, which is purposively focused on awareness of one's thinking and how one learns.
