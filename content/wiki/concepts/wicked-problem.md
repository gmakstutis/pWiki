# Wicked Problem

## Definition

A complex, multi-faceted problem for which no single elegant solution exists, and which cannot be addressed in isolation with a 'one size fits all', quick fix, or definitive solution. Wicked problems are characterised by the centrality of social relationships and interactions, and require complex, holistic approaches with multiple interventions at different structural levels. In higher education, student dissatisfaction with assessment and feedback is framed as a 'wicked' problem (Grint, 2008), in contrast to a 'tame' problem for which a straightforward solution exists.
