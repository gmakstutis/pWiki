# Professional Development

## Definition
The ongoing process of developing the skills, knowledge, and competencies of professionals. It can take many forms, including communities of practice, collaborative learning, training, workshops, and reflective practice. In higher education, professional development is often linked to improving teaching effectiveness and is supported by competency frameworks and training for teaching staff.
