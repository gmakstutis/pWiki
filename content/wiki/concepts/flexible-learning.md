# Flexible Learning

## Definition
A comprehensive approach to learning that gives students choice in "how, what, when and where [individuals] learn: the pace, place and mode of delivery" (HEA, 2015). The key elements are pace (accelerated, part-time, credit for prior learning), place (classroom, home, mobile, work-based), and mode (delivery method). Flexible learning enables institutions to be more responsive and relevant to diverse student populations and is a critical strategy for increasing access to higher education.
