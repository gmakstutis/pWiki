# Student Engagement

## Definition

The extent to which students meaningfully participate in learning experiences within the classroom, leading to the attainment of desired learning outcomes. The concept is contested: some definitions emphasize the quality of effort students devote to educationally purposeful activities (Hu & Kuh, 2002), others the process by which institutions attempt to involve students in shaping their learning experience (HEFCE, 2008).
