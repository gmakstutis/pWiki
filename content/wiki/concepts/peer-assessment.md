# Peer Assessment

## Definition

The assessment of student work by fellow students, increasingly incorporated into the assessment of the group work process. There is evidence that, if well planned, peer assessment can reduce free-riding. However, students often perceive peer assessment as procedurally unjust, and combining peer learning and peer assessment asks a lot of students, particularly given the competitive atmosphere common in higher education.
