# Stereotype Threat

## Definition

Being at risk of confirming, as self-characteristic, a negative stereotype about one's group. The existence of a negative stereotype about a group to which one belongs means that in situations where the stereotype is applicable, one is at risk of confirming it as a self-characterization, both to oneself and to others who know the stereotype. Stereotype threat is experienced essentially as a self-evaluative threat, and can befall anyone with a group identity about which some negative stereotype exists. For the person to be threatened in this way, he need not even believe the stereotype; he need only know that it stands as a hypothesis about him in situations where the stereotype is relevant. Stereotype threat can impair intellectual test performance and, over time, pressure students to protectively disidentify with achievement.
