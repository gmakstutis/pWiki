# Human Capital

## Definition
The stock of skills, knowledge, and abilities that workers possess, which can be increased through investment in education and training. Human capital theory traditionally holds that firms will pay only for firm-specific skills, since gains from general skills accrue to the worker. However, amendments to this theory show employers do have incentives to finance general training, partly because firm-specific and general skills are complementary.
