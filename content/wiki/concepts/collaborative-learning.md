# Collaborative Learning

## Definition

Learning that engages small groups of people who encourage each other to maximize their own and each other's learning. Collaborative learning engages learners in knowledge sharing, inspiring each other, depending upon each other, and applying active social interaction in a small group. "Good learning, like good work, is collaborative and social, not competitive and isolated." In collaborative learning communities there are four important issues that must be considered: empowering learners, building communities, continuing support, and being patient.
