# Feedback

## Definition

Information provided to a learner or practitioner about their performance, intended to enable improvement. In peer observation, feedback is the mechanism that encourages and enables the observed lecturer to identify and develop weaker elements of their teaching. Effective feedback should be immediate, informal, specific, balanced between positive and negative, and phrased subjectively ("It seemed to me...") rather than judgmentally ("This was bad...").
