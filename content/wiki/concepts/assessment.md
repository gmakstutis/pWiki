# Assessment

## Definition

The process of measuring and evaluating student learning, knowledge, skills, and competencies. Assessment can be formative (aimed at yielding results immediately useful in guiding subsequent action) or summative (aimed at responding to accountability demands and judging performance for a decision or record). Assessment of learning outcomes in higher education faces pressures for accountability data and consumer information, but existing ratings and rankings tend to neglect learning outcomes in favor of inputs, activities, and research outputs.
