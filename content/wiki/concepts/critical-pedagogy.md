# Critical Pedagogy

## Definition
A radical approach to education that seeks to transform oppressive structures in society using democratic and activist approaches to teaching and learning. A fundamental premise of neomarxist critical pedagogy is that systems of formal education function, in part, to maintain and reproduce an exploitative capitalist system. The most significant defining feature of critical pedagogy is its emphasis on the emancipatory potential of education. Any serious application must take steps to facilitate greater dialogue, critique, counter hegemony, and praxis.
