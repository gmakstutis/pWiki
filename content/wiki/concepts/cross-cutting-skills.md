# Cross-Cutting Skills

## Definition

Skills that cut across fields of study, including written and oral communication, teamwork, ethical decision-making, critical thinking, and the ability to apply knowledge in real-life situations. They are not discipline or career specific, can and should be developed within educational settings, and prepare individuals for lifelong learning in a rapidly changing global world. They have been referred to as soft skills, essential learning outcomes, boundary-crossing skills, or 21st century skills.
