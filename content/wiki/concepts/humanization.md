# Humanization

## Definition

The ontological vocation of human beings to become more fully human, which is constantly negated by dehumanization — the distortion of this vocation that marks not only those whose humanity has been stolen, but also (though in a different way) those who have stolen it. Dehumanization is a concrete historical fact but not a given destiny; it is the result of an unjust order that engenders violence in the oppressors, which in turn dehumanizes the oppressed. The great humanistic and historical task of the oppressed is to liberate themselves and their oppressors as well. Authentic liberation — the process of humanization — is not another deposit to be made in people; liberation is a praxis: the action and reflection of men and women upon their world in order to transform it.
