# Intended Learning Outcomes

## Definition

The desired outcomes of teaching, specified in terms not only of topic content but of the level of understanding students are expected to achieve. ILOs should be stated in terms that require students to demonstrate their understanding, using appropriate verbs (high level verbs include reflect, hypothesise, solve unseen complex problems; low level verbs include describe, identify, memorise). The same verbs need to be embedded in the teaching/learning activities and in the assessment tasks.
