# Programme Assessment

## Definition

Assessment considered at the level of the whole academic programme, rather than individual modules. Taking a programmatic approach and scaffolding the development of skills over the course of a student's studies supports both skill development and assessment literacy. Interventions at the programme and/or institutional level are more likely to be effective than module-level interventions.
