# Social Capital

## Definition
The networks, relationships, and community resources that individuals can draw upon to facilitate transitions and opportunities. In education, social capital can enable transitions to VET and beyond, but discussions of the social capital available to particular ethnic groups can readily fold into a deficit model that resonates with essentialist constructions of racialized groups. Related concepts include 'ethnic capital' (Modood), which serves as a community resource.
