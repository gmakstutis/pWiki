# Banking Concept of Education

## Definition
Freire's term for the model of education in which knowledge is a gift bestowed by those who consider themselves knowledgeable upon those whom they consider to know nothing. Projecting an absolute ignorance onto others, a characteristic of the ideology of oppression, negates education and knowledge as processes of inquiry. In the banking concept, the teacher teaches and the students are taught; the teacher knows everything and the students know nothing; the teacher talks and the students listen meekly; the teacher is the Subject of the learning process while the pupils are mere objects. The banking concept regards people as adaptable, manageable beings, and serves the interests of the oppressors by minimizing students' creative power and stimulating their credulity.
