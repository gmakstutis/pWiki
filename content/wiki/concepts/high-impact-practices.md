# High Impact Practices

## Definition

Educational practices that have been shown to be associated with high-quality learning and development. The official list of eleven HIPs includes writing- and inquiry-intensive courses, collaborative assignments, ePortfolio, capstone courses, first-year seminars, common intellectual experiences, learning communities, undergraduate research, diversity/study away/global learning, service and community-based learning, and internships. The key elements of HIPs (Kuh & O'Donnell, 2013) are: interactions with faculty and peers about substantive matters; frequent, timely, and constructive feedback; significant investment of time and effort; performance expectations set at appropriately high levels; experiences with diversity; periodic, structured opportunities to reflect and integrate learning; opportunities to discover relevance through real-world applications; and public demonstration of competence.
