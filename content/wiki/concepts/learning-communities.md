# Learning Communities

## Definition
Groups of students who participate together in a shared academic experience, often through co-registration or block scheduling that enables students to take courses together (Tinto, 1998). In the UK, where degrees are more often single-subject based, students participate as a cohort, meaning learning communities are already being adopted. Learning communities critically affect students' academic and social integration, and can be academically oriented (lecture, tutorial, laboratory and help sessions) or socially oriented (study groups, roommates, student societies, accommodation communities).
