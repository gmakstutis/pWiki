# Reflective Practice

## Definition

The deliberate and active process of considering one's beliefs, knowledge, and practice in light of the grounds that support them. Dewey (1933) distinguished reflection from passive recall, describing it as "an active, persistent and careful consideration of any belief or supposed form of knowledge." Schön (1983) distinguished "reflection on action" (deliberate, retrospective) from "reflection in action" (thinking on one's feet). Boud, Keogh, and Walker (1985) argued that reflection must account for emotions to fully realize learning.
