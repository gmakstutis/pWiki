# Ungrading

## Definition
The practice of reducing or eliminating grades from the educational system, based on the argument that grades undermine learning. Ungrading is not intended as a finish line or a recipe to be replicated; it represents a bottom-up transformation that must be acted upon, monitored, and analyzed to benefit students' learning. It requires constant collaboration, negotiation, and consensus among main stakeholders. Alternative approaches include pass/fail systems, students' assigning grades, and grade abolition.
