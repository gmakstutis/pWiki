# Summative Assessment

## Definition
Assessment aimed at responding to accountability demands of some organizationally higher authority, involving judging performance for a decision or record. At the individual level, it can have a sorting function, separating qualified from unqualified students. At the program or institutional level, aggregated results may be used as part of a national or state accountability framework. Only Brazil and some federal US states make accreditation and resource allocation directly contingent on satisfactory results in standardized outcomes assessments.
