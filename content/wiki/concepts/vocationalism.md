# Vocationalism

## Definition

The orientation of education toward the development of workplace skills, knowledge, and dispositions. An expansive understanding of vocational education and training is not only concerned with the development of workplace skills but also conceives of occupations having a broader civic and democratic remit. Vocationalism is closely tied to questions of class, gender, and social justice, and to the relationship between education and the labour market.
