# Problem Solving

## Definition
The ability to use basic facts and concepts flexibly to deal with new situations, predict various consequences, and solve problems. In classroom situations, problem solving can be viewed from two angles: as a way of thinking (of interest to psychologists) and as a way of teaching (of interest to teachers). Developing problem solving ability is gradually becoming a primary goal of education at school.
