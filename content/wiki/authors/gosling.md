# David Gosling

## Bio Sketch
Educational developer and researcher in higher education, known for his work on peer observation of teaching. He developed the influential "Three Models of Peer Observation of Teaching" (2002) and deconstructed the terms "peer," "observation," and "teaching" to clarify the nature of the practice.

## Key Works
- "Three Models of Peer Observation of Teaching" (2002) — distinguishes developmental, evaluative, and collaborative models of peer observation
