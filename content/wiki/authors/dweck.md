# Carol Dweck

## Bio Sketch
American psychologist and professor at Stanford University, best known for her research on mindsets — the beliefs people hold about whether intelligence and other qualities are fixed or can be developed. Her work has been highly influential in education, showing how the growth mindset promotes learning, resilience, and achievement.

## Key Works
- *Mindset: The New Psychology of Success* (2006) — the fixed and growth mindsets and their consequences
