# Andrea Cameron

## Bio Sketch
Researcher in the Division of Sport and Exercise Sciences at Abertay University, Dundee, Scotland. Her work investigates learning gain, widening participation, and graduate outcomes in sports degrees, using longitudinal educational outcomes to examine the impact of curriculum on employability.

## Key Works
- "An Investigation into the Comparative Learning Gain and 'Value Added' for Students from Widening Participation and Non-Widening Participation Groups: A Case Study from Sports Degrees" (2018, with Yvette Wharton and Jennifer B. Scally) — a case study of graduate outcomes for WP and non-WP students
