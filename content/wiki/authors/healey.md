# Mick Healey

## Bio Sketch
British geographer and higher education researcher known for his work on the research-teaching nexus and the scholarship of teaching and learning. With Alan Jenkins, he developed the influential four-way typology of linking research and teaching.

## Key Works
- *Developing Undergraduate Research and Enquiry* (2009, with Alan Jenkins) — the four-way typology: research-led, research-oriented, research-based, research-tutored
