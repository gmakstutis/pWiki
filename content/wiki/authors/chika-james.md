# Theresa Chika-James

## Bio Sketch

Researcher in Organizational Behavior, Human Resources and Management at MacEwan University, Edmonton, Canada. Her work examines service-learning and management education, with a focus on the relational competencies of faculty that facilitate effective service-learning.

## Key Works

- "Facilitating Service-Learning through Competencies Associated with Relational Pedagogy: A Personal Reflection" (2020) — a reflective case study of four relational competencies (care, interpersonal communication, an attentive presence, and trust) in a change management service-learning course
