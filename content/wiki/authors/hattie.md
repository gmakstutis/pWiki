# John Hattie

## Bio Sketch
Professor of Education at the University of Auckland, New Zealand. He is best known for his synthesis of over 500 meta-analyses of influences on student achievement, and for his work on feedback, assessment, and models of teaching and learning. His research interests include assessment, models of teaching and learning, and statistics.

## Key Works
- "The Power of Feedback" (2007, with Helen Timperley) — a conceptual analysis of feedback and a model of effective feedback
- *Visible Learning* (2009) — a synthesis of meta-analyses of influences on student achievement
