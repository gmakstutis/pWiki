# Noel Entwistle

## Bio Sketch

British educational psychologist who led the Lancaster Group's research on student learning, developing the Approaches to Studying Inventory (ASI). His work identified distinctive orientations to studying (meaning, reproducing, achieving, non-academic) and explored the relationship between learning approaches, personality, and academic performance.

## Key Works

- _Understanding Student Learning_ (1983, with Paul Ramsden) — the Lancaster Group's second project
- _Degrees of Excellence: The Academic Achievement Game_ (1977, with J.D. Wilson) — the Lancaster Group's first project
