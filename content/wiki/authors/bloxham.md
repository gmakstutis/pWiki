# Sue Bloxham

## Bio Sketch
Professor of academic practice and head of the Centre for the Development of Learning and Teaching at the University of Cumbria. She has been active in the development and research of assessment in higher education, particularly marking, moderation, and the role of assessment criteria in grading practices.

## Key Works
- "Marking and Moderation in the UK: False Assumptions and Wasted Resources" (2009) — challenges assumptions underlying marking
- "Mark My Words: The Role of Assessment Criteria in UK Higher Education Grading Practices" (2011, with Peter Boyd and Susan Orr) — on the gap between policy and practice in criteria-based grading
- *Developing Effective Assessment in Higher Education: A Practical Guide* (2007, with Pete Boyd)
