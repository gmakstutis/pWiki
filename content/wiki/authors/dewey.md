# John Dewey

## Bio Sketch

American philosopher, psychologist, and educational reformer (1859–1952), often considered the founder of reflective practice. His pragmatist philosophy of education emphasized learning through experience and the deliberate, active process of reflection.

## Key Works

- _How We Think: A Restatement of the Relation of Reflective Thinking to the Educative Process_ (1933) — distinguishes passive recall from reflection as a deliberate and active process

## Relevance to Paulo's Research

Dewey's account of reflection as an active, persistent consideration of belief in light of its grounds connects to Paulo's interest in the epistemological dimensions of practice and the productive role of experience in knowledge formation.
