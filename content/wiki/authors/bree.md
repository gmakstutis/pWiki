# Ronan Bree

## Bio Sketch
Academic in the Department of Life and Health Sciences at Dundalk Institute of Technology, Ireland. He was a co-author of the institute-wide framework for assessed group work, developed in response to feedback from students and staff.

## Key Works
- "An Institute-Wide Framework for Assessed Group Work: Development and Initial Implementation in an Irish Higher Education Institution" (2019, with Colin Cooney, Moira Maguire, Peter Morris and Paula Mullen)
