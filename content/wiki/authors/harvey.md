# Lee Harvey

## Bio Sketch
Higher education researcher and former Director of the Centre for Research into Quality at the University of Central England (now Birmingham City University). He has written extensively on employability, work experience, and the quality of higher education, including the influential Graduates' Work study.

## Key Works
- *Graduates' Work: Organisation Change and Students' Attributes* (1997, with Moon and Geall) — on employability and graduate attributes
- "Transforming Work Experience in Higher Education" (2001, with Blackwell, Bowes, Hesketh and Knight) — four empirical studies of work experience
