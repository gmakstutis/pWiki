# Joshua Cuevas

## Bio Sketch

Assistant Professor in the College of Education at the University of North Georgia, where he teaches courses in research methods, assessment, educational psychology, and literacy. He received a PhD in educational psychology from Georgia State University. His research interests include applied cognition, educational measurement, evidence-based reasoning, instructional methods, memory, and quantitative methodology.

## Key Works

- "Is Learning Styles-Based Instruction Effective? A Comprehensive Analysis of Recent Research on Learning Styles" (2015) — a comprehensive review of research on learning styles since Pashler et al. (2009)
