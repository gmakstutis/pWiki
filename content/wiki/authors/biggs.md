# John Biggs

## Bio Sketch
Educational psychologist who obtained his Ph.D. from the University of London in 1963 and held Chairs in Education in Canada, Australia, and Hong Kong. He is best known for developing the concept of constructive alignment and for his work on deep and surface approaches to learning, including the Study Process Questionnaire.

## Key Works
- *Teaching for Quality Learning at University* (2003) — the constructive alignment approach
- "Aligning Teaching for Constructing Learning" — on constructive alignment
- *Student Approaches to Learning and Studying* (1987) — the elaborated model of student learning
