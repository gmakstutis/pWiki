# Gordon Pask

## Bio Sketch

British cybernetician and psychologist who led the Richmond Group's research on how students learn complex new fields of knowledge. He identified serialist and holist learning strategies, and comprehension and operation learning styles, with associated pathologies of globetrotting and improvidence. He developed a conversation theory of learning.

## Key Works

- Research on learning strategies and styles (1972-1977, with Pask and Scott) — the Richmond Group's studies
