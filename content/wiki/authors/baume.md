# David Baume

## Bio Sketch
Fellow of the Centre for Distance Education at the University of London, and a higher education consultant and writer specializing in learning gain, assessment, and the scholarship of teaching and learning. He has written extensively on the measurement of learning and the deficiencies of current assessment practice.

## Key Works
- "Towards a Measure of Learning Gain. A Journey. With Obstacles" (2018) — an opinion piece on the difficulties of measuring learning gain
