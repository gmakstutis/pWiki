# David Kolb

## Bio Sketch
American educational theorist (b. 1939) best known for developing the experiential learning theory and the four-stage experiential learning cycle. His work has been foundational in higher education pedagogy, particularly for reflective practice and professional development.

## Key Works
- *Experiential Learning: Experience as the Source of Learning and Development* (1984) — the four-cycle framework: Concrete Experience, Reflective Observation, Abstract Conceptualisation, Active Implementation

## Relevance to Paulo's Research
Kolb's experiential learning cycle provides a structured model for reflection that connects experience to learning, relevant to Paulo's interest in the relationship between practice and knowledge in music composition and pedagogy.
