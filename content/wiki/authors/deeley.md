# Susan Deeley

## Bio Sketch

Researcher in the School of Social and Political Sciences at the University of Glasgow. Her work examines assessment and feedback in higher education, including summative co-assessment, service-learning, and the use of technology in assessment for learning.

## Key Works

- "Exploring the 'Wicked' Problem of Student Dissatisfaction with Assessment and Feedback in Higher Education" (2019, with Moira Fischbacher-Smith, Dimitar Karadzhov and Elina Koristashevskaya) — a qualitative study of student dissatisfaction with assessment and feedback
- "Summative Co-Assessment: A Deep Learning Approach to Enhancing Employability Skills and Attributes" (2014) — on co-assessment
- _Critical Perspectives on Service-Learning in Higher Education_ (2015)
