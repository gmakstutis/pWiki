# O. Osman Demirbas

## Bio Sketch

Researcher in the Department of Interior Architecture and Environmental Design, Faculty of Art, Design and Architecture at Bilkent University, Turkey. His work examines learning styles in design education, using Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory to explore the relationship between learning styles, gender and academic performance.

## Key Works

- "Learning Styles of Design Students and the Relationship of Academic Performance and Gender in Design Education" (2007, with Halime Demirkan) — a study of freshman design students across three academic years
