# Joshua Aronson

## Bio Sketch
Professor at the University of Texas, Austin, known for his research on stereotype threat and its effects on intellectual performance. He co-developed the concept of stereotype threat with Claude Steele.

## Key Works
- "Stereotype Threat and the Intellectual Test Performance of African Americans" (1995, with Claude Steele) — the foundational study of stereotype threat
