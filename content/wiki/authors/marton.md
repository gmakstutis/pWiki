# Ference Marton

## Bio Sketch
Swedish educational researcher who led the Swedish (Gothenburg) Group's research on deep and surface approaches to learning. His principal contribution was to emphasize that the intention of the student is crucial: what a student intends to get out of learning determines whether a deep or surface approach will be used. He utilized a phenomenographical approach.

## Key Works
- Research on learning approaches to and outcomes from reading academic articles (1975, with Säljö and others) — the Gothenburg studies
