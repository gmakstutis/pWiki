# Susan Blum

## Bio Sketch

Professor of anthropology at the University of Notre Dame, known for her work on education, learning, and the critique of grading. She edited the influential volume _Ungrading: Why Rating Students Undermines Learning (and What to Do Instead)_ (2020), which argues for the elimination of grades from the educational system.

## Key Works

- _Ungrading: Why Rating Students Undermines Learning (and What to Do Instead)_ (2020) — an edited volume on ungrading
