# Jason Cope

## Bio Sketch

Lecturer in Entrepreneurship at the Institute for Entrepreneurship and Enterprise Development, Lancaster University. His research examines entrepreneurial learning, particularly how entrepreneurs learn from significant, discontinuous events through critical reflection.

## Key Works

- "Entrepreneurial Learning and Critical Reflection: Discontinuous Events as Triggers for 'Higher-level' Learning" (2003) — a qualitative case study of six entrepreneurs showing how discontinuous events trigger double-loop and transformative learning
