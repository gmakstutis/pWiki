# Robert Lerman

## Bio Sketch

American economist affiliated with the Urban Institute, American University, and IZA, specializing in labor economics, vocational education, and apprenticeship training. He is a leading advocate for the expansion of apprenticeship systems as a means of improving worker skills and reducing youth unemployment.

## Key Works

- "Do Firms Benefit from Apprenticeship Investments?" (2019) — IZA World of Labor article on the economics of apprenticeship
- _Skill Development in Middle Level Occupations: The Role of Apprenticeship Training_ (2013) — IZA Policy Paper
