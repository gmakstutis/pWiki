# Helen Timperley

## Bio Sketch
Professor of Education at the University of Auckland, New Zealand. Her research investigates how to promote learning at all levels of the education system, including professional development and feedback. She co-authored with John Hattie and Shirley Clarke the book *Unlocking Formative Assessment*.

## Key Works
- "The Power of Feedback" (2007, with John Hattie) — a conceptual analysis of feedback and a model of effective feedback
- *Unlocking Formative Assessment* (2003, with John Hattie and Shirley Clarke)
