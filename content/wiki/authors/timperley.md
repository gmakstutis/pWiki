# Helen Timperley

## Bio Sketch

Professor of Education at the University of Auckland, New Zealand. Her research investigates how to promote learning at all levels of the education system, including professional development and feedback. She co-authored with John Hattie and Shirley Clarke the book _Unlocking Formative Assessment_.

## Key Works

- "The Power of Feedback" (2007, with John Hattie) — a conceptual analysis of feedback and a model of effective feedback
- _Unlocking Formative Assessment_ (2003, with John Hattie and Shirley Clarke)
