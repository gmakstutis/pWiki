# Manica Danko

## Bio Sketch
Researcher at the University of Ljubljana, Faculty of Administration (Slovenia). Her work examines peer observation of teaching (POT) in higher education, including the inclusion of students in the observation practice to enhance the awareness of student experience in teaching and learning.

## Key Works
- "Peer Observation in Higher Education as an Agent of Change in Teaching and Learning" (2016, with Damijana Keržič and Žiga Kotnik) — a pilot project of peer observation of teaching at the Faculty of Administration
