# Alexandros Chrysikos

## Bio Sketch

Researcher in the School of Computing and Digital Media at London Metropolitan University. His work examines student retention, learning communities, and the academic and social integration of first-year undergraduate computing students in UK higher education.

## Key Works

- "Identifying Student Retention Factors of a UK University Using the Concept of a Learning Community: A Qualitative Approach" (2020, with Stephen Catterall) — a qualitative study using the 'unfolding matrix' to examine learning communities and retention
