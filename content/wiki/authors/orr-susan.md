# Susan Orr

## Bio Sketch

Higher education researcher in the Faculty of Arts at York St John University, specializing in assessment, marking, and moderation. Her work examines the social construction of marks and the role of assessment in higher education, including the concept of 'validating practices'.

## Key Works

- "Assessment Moderation: Constructing the Marks and Constructing the Students" (2007) — on the social construction of marks
- "Mark My Words: The Role of Assessment Criteria in UK Higher Education Grading Practices" (2011, with Sue Bloxham and Peter Boyd)
