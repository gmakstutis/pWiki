# Donald Schön

## Bio Sketch

American philosopher and professor (1930–1997) who made a more explicit link between professional development and professional practice, building on Dewey's work. His concept of the "reflective practitioner" has been highly influential in education and professional training.

## Key Works

- _The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action_ (1983) — distinguishes "reflection on action" from "reflection in action"

## Relevance to Paulo's Research

Schön's distinction between reflection in and on action is relevant to Paulo's interest in the tacit knowledge underlying musical and compositional practice, and how practitioners make tacit knowledge known.
