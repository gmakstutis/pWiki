# Peter Knight

## Bio Sketch

Higher education researcher at Lancaster University, specializing in assessment, learning, and employability. He has written extensively on the local practices of assessment, the value of a programme-wide approach to assessment, and the relationship between assessment, learning, and employability.

## Key Works

- _Assessment, Learning and Employability_ (2003, with Mantz Yorke) — on assessment and employability
- "The Local Practices of Assessment" (2006) — on the situated nature of assessment standards
