# Jenny Byrne

## Bio Sketch

Academic in the School of Education at the University of Southampton, specializing in education and professional development. She was a co-author of the peer development process, introduced as an alternative to traditional peer observation of teaching.

## Key Works

- "Peer Development as an Alternative to Peer Observation: A Tool to Enhance Professional Development" (2010, with Hazel Brown and Doreen Challen)
