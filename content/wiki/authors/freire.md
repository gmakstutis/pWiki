# Paulo Freire

## Bio Sketch

Brazilian educator and philosopher (1921–1997), one of the most influential educational theorists of the twentieth century. He is the foundational theorist of critical pedagogy, arguing that education should be a practice of freedom that enables the oppressed to transform their conditions. His work emphasizes dialogue, praxis, and the critique of the "banking" model of education.

## Key Works

- _Pedagogy of the Oppressed_ (1970) — the foundational text of critical pedagogy
- _Education for Critical Consciousness_ (1974) — on dialogue and the culture of silence
