# Martin Compton

## Bio Sketch
Academic Developer at the University of Greenwich, with a background in further education teacher training. His doctoral research (EdD, UCL Institute of Education, 2019) examined unorthodox approaches to observation of teaching and learning (OTL) in higher education, using Activity Theory as an analytical framework. He developed the '4 Ms Observation Audit' (Milieu, Motive, Mediator, Mechanism) as a tool for appraising and designing OTL schemes.

## Key Works
- "Rethinking Professional Development Observations of HE Lecturers: Cases of the Unorthodox" (2019) — a doctoral thesis on three unorthodox approaches to OTL
