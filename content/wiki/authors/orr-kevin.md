# Kevin Orr

## Bio Sketch
Professor at the University of Huddersfield, specializing in further education, vocational education and training, and teacher education in the FE sector. His work examines the relationship between education, class, and social justice, and the professionalization of FE teachers.

## Key Works
- "HE in FE: Vocationalism, Class and Social Justice" (2016, with James Avis) — examines HE in FE and social mobility
- "Black Students in VET: Learner Experiences in an English Metropolitan and Provincial Setting" (2018, with James Avis and Paul Warmington) — on the lived experience of black students in VET
