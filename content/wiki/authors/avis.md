# James Avis

## Bio Sketch
Professor at the University of Huddersfield, specializing in the sociology of education, vocational education and training, and the political economy of education. His work examines the relationship between education, class, and social justice, particularly in further education and vocational education.

## Key Works
- "HE in FE: Vocationalism, Class and Social Justice" (2016, with Kevin Orr) — examines HE in FE and social mobility
- *Social Justice, Transformation and Knowledge: Policy, Workplace Learning and Skills* (2016) — on social justice and vocational education
