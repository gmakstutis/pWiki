# Deborah Nusche

## Bio Sketch
Policy analyst in the Education and Training Policy Division of the OECD Directorate for Education, specializing in higher education policy, learning outcomes, and assessment. She prepared the OECD Education Working Paper on the assessment of learning outcomes in higher education as a consultant to the Indicators and Analysis Division.

## Key Works
- "Assessment of Learning Outcomes in Higher Education: A Comparative Review of Selected Practices" (2008) — OECD Education Working Paper No. 15
