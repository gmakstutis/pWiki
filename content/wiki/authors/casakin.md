# Hernan Casakin

## Bio Sketch

Lecturer in the Department of Architecture at Ariel University Center of Samaria and in the Porter School of Environmental Studies, Tel Aviv University. His research interests include architectural and urban design from an interdisciplinary perspective: creativity, cognition, visual reasoning, education and sociology. He works on the assessment of creativity and its effect on students' performance in the architectural design studio.

## Key Works

- "Individual Learning Styles and Design Performance in the Metaphorical Reasoning Process" (2008, with Kevin Miller) — a theoretical study proposing hypotheses on the relationship between Kolb's learning styles and the phases of metaphorical reasoning in design
