# Pierre Bourdieu

## Bio Sketch
French sociologist (1930–2002) whose work on cultural capital, habitus, field, and social reproduction has been highly influential in the sociology of education. His analysis of how education reproduces social inequality is central to debates about widening participation and social mobility.

## Key Works
- *Reproduction in Education, Society and Culture* (1970, with Jean-Claude Passeron) — on how education reproduces social inequality
- *Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste* (1979) — on cultural capital and taste
