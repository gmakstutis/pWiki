# Pierre Bourdieu

## Bio Sketch

French sociologist (1930–2002) whose work on cultural capital, habitus, field, and social reproduction has been highly influential in the sociology of education. His analysis of how education reproduces social inequality is central to debates about widening participation and social mobility.

## Key Works

- _Reproduction in Education, Society and Culture_ (1970, with Jean-Claude Passeron) — on how education reproduces social inequality
- _Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste_ (1979) — on cultural capital and taste

Bourdieu's concepts of cultural capital, habitus, and field are relevant to Paulo's interest in how knowledge and practice are distributed and reproduced, and how education relates to social structures.
