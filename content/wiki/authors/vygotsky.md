# Lev Vygotsky

## Bio Sketch
Soviet psychologist (1896–1934) whose sociocultural theory of learning emphasized the social and collaborative dimensions of cognitive development. He is best known for the concept of the Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD), the gap between what a learner can do independently and what they can achieve with guidance.

## Key Works
- *Mind in Society* (1978) — the development of higher psychological processes and the Zone of Proximal Development
