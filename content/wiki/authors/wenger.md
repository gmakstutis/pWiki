# Etienne Wenger

## Bio Sketch
Swiss-American educational theorist and practitioner, best known for co-developing the concept of communities of practice with Jean Lave. His work on situated learning and social learning theory has been highly influential in education, organizational learning, and professional development.

## Key Works
- *Situated Learning: Legitimate Peripheral Participation* (1991, with Jean Lave) — the foundational text on communities of practice
- *Cultivating Communities of Practice* (2002, with Richard McDermott and William Snyder) — practical guidance on communities of practice
