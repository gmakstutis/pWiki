# Gloria Ladson-Billings

## Bio Sketch
Professor at the University of Wisconsin, a leading scholar in education and critical race theory. She is best known for developing the concept of culturally relevant pedagogy and for applying critical race theory to education, using race as an analytic tool for understanding school inequity.

## Key Works
- "Toward a Critical Race Theory of Education" (1995, with William F. Tate IV) — the foundational application of critical race theory to education
- *The Dreamkeepers: Successful Teachers of African American Children* (1994) — on culturally relevant pedagogy
