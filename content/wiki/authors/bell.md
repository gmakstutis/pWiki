# Alex Bell

## Bio Sketch
Liaison Librarian at the University for the Creative Arts. He co-authored a study on inclusive grading in creative arts higher education as part of a PgCert Creative Education programme, examining the impact of grading on student motivation, mental health, and mindset.

## Key Works
- "How Can Grading in Creative Arts Higher Education Be More Inclusive to Facilitate Learning?" (2024, with Hrefna Bragadottir, Liam Lacey, Preeti Sood and Rob Payne) — a mixed-method study of inclusive grading in creative education
