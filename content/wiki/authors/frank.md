# Jason Frank

## Bio Sketch
Associate Director of the Office of Education at the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada, and Director of Education in the Department of Emergency Medicine at the University of Ottawa. He is a leading figure in competency-based medical education (CBME), having convened the International CBME Collaborators to examine conceptual issues and current debates in the field.

## Key Works
- "Competency-Based Medical Education: Theory to Practice" (2010, with Linda Snell, Olle ten Cate, et al.) — a consensus paper proposing definitions and concepts for CBME
