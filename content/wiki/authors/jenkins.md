# Alan Jenkins

## Bio Sketch

British higher education researcher known for his work on the research-teaching nexus and curriculum design. With Mick Healey, he developed the four-way typology of linking research and teaching and coined the term "teaching-research nexus."

## Key Works

- _Developing Undergraduate Research and Enquiry_ (2009, with Mick Healey) — the four-way typology
- "Research-led or research-based undergraduate curricula" (2013, with Mick Healey) — the "teaching-research" nexus
