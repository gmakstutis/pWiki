# Michel Foucault

## Bio Sketch
French philosopher and historian of ideas (1926–1984), whose work on power, discipline, discourse, and the archaeology of knowledge has been highly influential across the humanities and social sciences. His analysis of the "disciplinary society" and normalising judgements is relevant to the critique of assessment and surveillance in education.

## Key Works
- *Discipline and Punish* (1975) — the disciplinary society and normalising judgements
- *The Archaeology of Knowledge* (1969) — discourse and the archaeology of knowledge
