# Maureen Snow Andrade

## Bio Sketch

Higher education researcher and professor in the Organizational Leadership Department at Utah Valley University, specializing in flexible learning, online and blended learning, cross-cutting skills, and self-regulated learning. She has developed frameworks for scalable expansion of flexible learning and strategies for teaching cross-cutting skills.

## Key Works

- "Developing a Framework for Sustainable Growth of Flexible Learning Opportunities" (2019, with Bethany Alden-Rivers) — the Framework for Flexible Learning
- "Cross-Cutting Skills: Strategies for Teaching & Learning" (2020) — HIPs and self-regulated learning in course design
