# Antonio Gramsci

## Bio Sketch

Italian Marxist philosopher and political theorist (1891–1937), best known for developing the concept of hegemony. His work on how ruling classes maintain power through consent and ideology, rather than force alone, has been highly influential in critical pedagogy and cultural studies.

## Key Works

- _Selections from the Prison Notebooks_ (1971) — the concept of hegemony
