# Margaret Price

## Bio Sketch
Higher education researcher in the Department of Business and Management at Oxford Brookes University, specializing in assessment, feedback, and the scholarship of assessment. Her work examines assessment standards, the role of communities of practice in assessment, and the use of assessment criteria.

## Key Works
- "Let's Stop the Pretence of Consistent Marking: Exploring the Multiple Limitations of Assessment Criteria" (2015, with Sue Bloxham, Birgit den Outer and Jane Hudson)
- "Assessment Standards: The Role of Communities of Practice and the Scholarship of Assessment" (2005)
