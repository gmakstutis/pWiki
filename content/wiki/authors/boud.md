# David Boud

## Bio Sketch

Higher education researcher and professor at Deakin University, the University of Technology Sydney, and Middlesex University, specializing in assessment, feedback, and learning. He is a leading figure in the field of assessment for learning and the critique of assessment practice.

## Key Works

- "Assessment Could Demonstrate Learning Gains, but What is Required for It to Do So?" (2018) — on assessment and learning gain
- _Reflection: Turning Experience into Learning_ (1985, with Keogh and Walker) — on reflection
- "Assessment and the Promotion of Academic Values" (1990) — on assessment
